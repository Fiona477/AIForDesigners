01 Campaign Brief

Cool in the City

A crisp, pale-green break from the heat, the noise and the grind of New York.

Diet Coke Honeydew is a limited-edition flavor that pairs the classic Diet Coke taste with the light, fresh sweetness of honeydew melon. The campaign launches in New York City and positions the drink as the city's coolest pause: the moment of calm between the subway platform and the next meeting.

Visually, the campaign mixes Diet Coke's clean silver can with soft honeydew greens, city textures like subway tile, chrome and concrete, and bright summer light. It should feel fresh, confident and a little playful, never sugary or loud.

  • ProductDiet Coke Honeydew, zero sugar, 12 oz can
  • MarketNew York City, all five boroughs
  • AudienceNew Yorkers 18–34: students, creatives and commuters
  • LaunchSpring 2027
  • MoodCrisp, fresh, calm, confident, city-cool
  • TouchpointsSubway posters, billboards, wheat-paste walls, phone reels and stories
