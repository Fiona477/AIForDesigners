Image Generation Process

From a simple chair to a warm NYC living space.

Generation 1
A chair in the middle of a room.
Generation 2
A lounge chair in the middle of a NYC apartment room.
Generation 3
A lounge chair that is fluffy. Think big and comfortable.
Generation 4
Make the time of day morning, with sunlight hitting the chair. The room should give off a warm feel.
Generation 5
Make the image shot from a higher angle, like from the wall. Make sure the furniture and windows are still visible.
